ActivationRBI card statisticsJan–Sep 2026

Card Pulse: banks are issuing cards faster than people use them

Signal
India added 8.65 million credit cards between January and September 2026, but monthly spend per card slipped from ₹17,145 to ₹16,281.
Problem
New cardholders aren't building a habit. Each dormant card costs the bank acquisition spend and earns nothing.
Product
A 90-day activation journey: a first-spend nudge, a category-based welcome offer and a UPI-linked option for small daily payments.
Next
10 interviews with new cardholders who rarely use their card. Guide and hypotheses ready.

Payments strategyNPCI UPI statisticsJan–Oct 2026

UPI × Credit: where card issuers should meet small-ticket spending

Signal
UPI hit a record 25.47 billion transactions in October 2026 while its average ticket fell to about ₹1,238. Every month it moves roughly 14× the value of all credit-card spending.
Problem
Everyday spending happens at the QR code, but only RuPay credit cards can run on UPI. Issuers on other networks miss that frequency.
Product
A "credit on QR" proposition for a premium issuer: a RuPay companion card, rewards tuned for sub-₹2,000 spends and one shared credit limit.
Next
A fake-door test of the riskiest belief: do premium cardholders want rewards on small QR payments?

Customer experienceRBI Ombudsman reportFY 2024-25

Complaint Radar: designing credit-card disputes out of the ombudsman queue

Signal
Credit cards became the second-largest complaint category at the RBI Ombudsman in FY25, with 50,811 complaints, up 20% in a year.
Problem
Customers escalate when they can't see what is happening to a dispute, refund or charge. I answered these questions every day in customer care.
Product
An in-app dispute tracker with stage-by-stage status, SLA countdowns and automatic refund of credit balances, the issue behind an RBI penalty on one issuer in 2025.
Built
A clickable prototype of the tracker, with a 5-task usability test plan.
