# UPI × Credit: where card issuers should meet small-ticket spending

**Signal.** UPI processed a record 25.47 bn transactions worth ₹31.53 lakh crore in October 2026. The average payment fell from ₹1,306 (Jan) to ₹1,238 (Oct), and each month UPI moves 13.5–15.2× the value of all credit-card spending.

**Product.** A "credit on QR" companion: a virtual RuPay credit card on the same limit as a premium card, capped small-spend rewards, one statement and customer-set spend controls.

**Metrics.** North star: monthly active days across both cards. Guardrails: reward cost per transaction, fraud loss rate, delinquency.

Files: `index.html` (dashboard) · `data.csv` · `analysis.py` (reads `../card-pulse/data.csv` too)

Source: NPCI monthly UPI statistics as reported in the press; Bernstein via Mint; Equirus via Kotak Neo.
