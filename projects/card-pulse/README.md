# Card Pulse: banks are issuing credit cards faster than people use them

**Signal.** India's cards outstanding rose 7.4% between January and September 2026 (116.65 mn → 125.30 mn), while monthly spend per card fell from ₹17,145 to ₹16,281. In September 2026 spend per card was down 14.8% year on year.

**Product.** A first-90-days activation journey for new cardholders: first-spend nudge, pick-your-category welcome offer, a small-ticket UPI rail for RuPay variants and a plain-language first bill.

**Metrics.** North star: spend per card in the first 90 days. Guardrails: 90-day delinquency, complaints per 10,000 new cards, reward cost per activated card.

**Discovery plan.** Three hypotheses with how each will be tested, and a 20-minute interview guide for 10 new cardholders (on the dashboard page; interviews not yet run).

Files: `index.html` (dashboard) · `data.csv` · `issuers_apr2026.csv` · `analysis.py` (`python analysis.py`)

Source: RBI bank-wise ATM/POS/card statistics as reported by Business Standard, Angel One, Outlook Money, Tribune and Moneycontrol.
